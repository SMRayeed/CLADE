\section{Three Fixes}
\label{app:fixes}

This appendix gives the per-model numbers behind \cref{M-fig:fixes} of the main paper.

\paragraph{Confidence.}
A simple guard is to answer only when the model is confident. \Cref{tab:conf} shows, for each model and page type, how many questions the model answers, its precision, its mean confidence on right and on wrong answers, and the AUROC (\cref{app:stats}). On named pages, right answers carry clearly more confidence than wrong ones, and AUROC is 0.73--0.87. On family-only pages the two are close, and AUROC drops to 0.59--0.72. The wrong guesses are confident: Qwen2.5-14B puts a mean probability of 0.99 on its wrong family-only answers. In GloBI the drop is larger, from 0.82--0.86 on named pages to 0.52--0.68.

\paragraph{Scale.}
Within Qwen2.5, a larger model is more precise on named pages: 84.5\% at 0.5B, 95.0\% at 7B and 96.0\% at 14B. On family-only pages, precision rises from 38.1\% to 59.4\% between 0.5B and 7B and then stops: the 14B model reaches 58.4\%. The 14B model gives 14.7 points more wrong answers on family-only pages than on silent ones ($p=6\times10^{-6}$). Like the 7B model, it already declines family-only questions less often closed book, so its page effect does not differ between the groups ($+5.3$ points, $p=0.29$). A larger model reads named pages better but still guesses from a family name.

\paragraph{Instruction.}
We add one sentence to the prompt with the page:
\begin{quote}
\small\emph{If the passage names only a broad category of diseases and does not say which specific disease is meant, answer with the letter of ``Not enough information to tell.}
\end{quote} \Cref{tab:instr} shows the result. The models react very differently. OLMo~2 declines far more on every page type, including named pages (from 7.5\% to 27.4\%). Granite barely reacts. Qwen2.5-7B declines somewhat more. Pooled over the three models, the instruction raises declines by 15.2 points on family-only pages, but also by 14.0 on silent pages and 9.1 on named pages, where it costs 6.3 points of correct answers. Precision on family-only pages stays low (47.9--64.9\%). The instruction makes the models more cautious everywhere, but it does not teach them to tell a family name from an answer.
